`src/shared/campaigns.ts` and `src/server/campaigns/generate.ts` define field lengths, criterion semantics and conversion to a stored campaign. `src/shared/schemas.ts` excludes meeting_booked from research/networking campaigns. These contracts override a book's suggested template length or call objective.

Our synthesis deliberately avoids invented client proof, rigid differentiator counts, mandatory budget gates for discovery, repeated pressure and universal numeric activity targets. Keenan's PIC is a hypothesis map at campaign creation; current state, impact and root cause remain to be confirmed. Offer − Ask = Value is qualitative, not numerical ROI. His criticism of early BANT gates does not remove actual affordability or capability constraints.

Blount's methods differ by stage: prospecting **Ledge → Disrupt → Ask**; micro-commitment **Ledge → Explain value → Ask**; buying commitment **Relate → Isolate and clarify → Minimize → Ask → Fall back to an alternative**. A cold-call campaign must not imply a purchase-stage discovery has happened. The source's persistence/PAIS and adversarial wording are bounded by Mantis's one-clarification/refusal rules, truthful claims and direct answers to material questions. Framework names preserve attribution, not unrestricted adoption of every tactic.

No additional book is required to complete this pack. *The Mom Test* and *SPIN Selling* remain optional future complements, not ingested sources.
