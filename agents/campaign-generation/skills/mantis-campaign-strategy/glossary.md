# Terms

- **Business / technical problem** — Keenan separates the organizational consequence from the process or technology issue; causality remains to be checked.
- **Client issue** — Buyer problem, pressure, opportunity or desired result; source of the sales story (Weinberg).
- **Criterion** — Observable condition the engine investigates, not an assertion about a lead.
- **Disqualifier** — Confirmed structural mismatch supported by the offering's actual scope.
- **Gap** — Difference between current and desired future state (Keenan), not guaranteed ROI.
- **Ledge → Explain value → Ask** — Blount's micro-commitment method for explaining the real benefit of a next action to an engaged buyer.
- **Offer − Ask = Value** — Keenan's qualitative test that buyer benefit warrants the requested time/effort.
- **onNo** — Campaign decision for a confirmed negative criterion: disqualified, defer or unknown.
- **Possible Value Proposition** — A reason the offering might matter that remains to be verified (Sobczak).
- **Power Statement** — Source sales story organized around client issues, offering and differentiators (Weinberg).
- **Primary objective** — Action sought from the contact; a secondary objective is a proportionate alternative (Sobczak).
- **Probing / process / provoking / validating** — Keenan's question purposes: specifics, how, a consequence/alternative and check understanding.
- **Problem Identification Chart (PIC)** — Keenan's map of problem, impact and root cause; campaign entries are hypotheses, not verified prospect pain.
- **Problem Proposition** — Triggering problem, one-sentence solution and interest-based CTA (Farrokh/Cegelski).
