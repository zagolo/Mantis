# Terms

- **Alternative explanation** — another plausible cause of the same observed event; used to prevent premature diagnosis.
- **Buying signal** — evidence that may increase relevance or timing; it is not proof of purchase intent.
- **Client issue** — a buyer's problem, pressure, opportunity or desired result (Weinberg).
- **CRMT (Client Research Management Tool)** — Richter's reusable record of external research and learned customer context; this skill maps it to existing brief fields and keeps evidence origins distinct ([ch04](chapters/ch04-handoff-and-feedback.md)).
- **Event date** — when the described change happened; different from when a page was fetched.
- **Fourth R** — research applied to relevant, informed business conversations (Richter; [ch02](chapters/ch02-find-and-verify.md), [ch05](chapters/ch05-purposeful-search.md)).
- **ICP fit** — match to the campaign's target criteria; unknown attributes remain unknown.
- **Industry context** — an attributed sector pattern used to ask whether it applies to the account; not proof of account-specific pain ([ch03](chapters/ch03-evidence-to-conversation.md)).
- **Invisible Web** — Richter's discussion of information missed by general search, including source-specific databases; no implication of unauthorized access or a current coverage percentage ([ch05](chapters/ch05-purposeful-search.md)).
- **Possible Value Proposition (PVP)** — a researched reason a prospect might benefit, requiring validation (Sobczak).
- **Problem Proposition** — concrete problem-focused messaging that earns a conversation (Farrokh/Cegelski).
- **Provenance** — the trace from a claim to its actual source and evidence.
- **Signal ranking** — campaign-specific search order that prioritizes stronger reasons to call (Farrokh/Cegelski).
- **Trigger event** — a change that may create a relevant need; the consequence still needs confirmation.
- **Warm call** — Richter's relevant, researched conversation; this skill does not use “warm” to imply an existing relationship or permission ([ch03](chapters/ch03-evidence-to-conversation.md)).
- **Webmaster Think** — Richter's method of searching with the terms a publisher would use to label the desired information ([ch05](chapters/ch05-purposeful-search.md)).
