# Prospect research and live cold-call coaching

Prepared 18 September 2026 for this Sales Engine. My recommendation is two combined skills with different jobs: research earns a defensible reason to call; coaching chooses the next useful sentence from what the prospect actually says. More source material helps only when it improves those decisions.

The [current readiness report](skill-readiness.md) records completed source integration, application invocation, model checks and remaining live-call limits.

## What is built

- [prospect-research-playbook](../../agents/prospect-research/skills/prospect-research-playbook/SKILL.md): account fit → identity → relevant evidence → possible problem → opener and disconfirming question.
- [live-cold-call-coach](../../agents/live-coach/skills/live-cold-call-coach/SKILL.md): truthful opener → concrete problem proposition → listening and discovery → respectful objection clarification → mutually useful next step.

Each has a compact runtime cheat sheet plus workflow chapters, techniques, glossary and source provenance. The engine explicitly selects one combined skill for each agent. Existing author skills remain available as references. Runtime changes invalidate old research briefs; long chapters and transcripts are not loaded during every call turn.

The four originals were supplied from the operator's private iCloud `Documents/salesbooks` library. The installed book-to-skill extractor processed them; selected passages were checked against existing generated analysis. This is a synthesis using prior analysis and targeted verification, not a claim that every page was reread. Extracted images, charts and audio-linked material were not comprehensively inspected.

**Source completion:** The user subsequently supplied *Take the Cold Out of Cold Calling*, *Objections* and *Gap Selling*. All three were extracted with book-to-skill and relevant sections were folded into the same compiled skills. Both five-book curricula now have all their source books: ten slots, seven unique books. This is targeted operational synthesis, not an assertion that every page or illustration was reviewed. See [Richter coverage](richter-book-foldin.md) and [Objections/Gap coverage](objections-gap-foldin.md).

## Five books for prospect research

| Rank | Book | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smart Calling — Art Sobczak** | Best bridge from company/person intelligence to a Possible Value Proposition and a relevant first question. |
| 2 | **Cold Calling Sucks (And That's Why It Works) — Armand Farrokh & Nick Cegelski** | Makes research operational: prioritize timing signals and turn them into a concrete call opening. |
| 3 | **Take the Cold Out of Cold Calling — Sam Richter** | The most concentrated research complement: purposeful web investigation and useful context. Older tools need current verification. |
| 4 | **New Sales. Simplified. — Mike Weinberg** | Stops wasted research on the wrong accounts; connects a focused target list to client issues and the sales story. |
| 5 | **Fanatical Prospecting — Jeb Blount** | Keeps preparation from consuming the time needed for conversations. Valuable discipline, although not primarily a research book. |

Read the first two for the research-to-call bridge, Richter for depth, Weinberg for targeting, and Blount for execution. The ranking is tailored to this job, not a universal bestseller ranking.

## Five books for live coaching

| Rank | Book | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold Calling Sucks — Farrokh & Cegelski** | The strongest mechanical spine: opener, Problem Proposition, objection conversation and meeting. |
| 2 | **Smart Calling — Art Sobczak** | Keeps the caller relevant, curious and prepared to listen; supports questions and mutual commitments. |
| 3 | **Objections — Jeb Blount** | Dedicated resistance and emotional-control training to complement the basic call structure. |
| 4 | **Gap Selling — Keenan** | Helps the caller discover whether a meaningful problem and reason to change exist. Use after the prospect is willing to talk. |
| 5 | **Fanatical Prospecting — Jeb Blount** | Call structure, composure, repetition and pipeline discipline. Activity advice stays outside the live cue. |

Keep *New Sales. Simplified.* as a shared campaign-design reference. Do not crowd the live skill with every sales methodology. For a complex AI service, the cold call normally earns a qualified discovery or pilot-scoping conversation; pricing, scope, success criteria and commercial agreement come when supported.

## My design choices for this engine

1. **Use evidence to choose the question.** A hiring page can establish vacancies. It cannot establish overload, budget or urgency. The prospect's answer supplies that missing information.
2. **Keep one coherent call method.** PVP determines possible relevance; Problem Proposition expresses it; one acknowledgement and clarification handles ambiguity. Do not concatenate every author's scripts.
3. **Let the buyer correct the brief.** If the vacancies are replacements, retire the growth hypothesis immediately. Research must never become a script the caller defends.
4. **Close the next justified step.** Meeting booked, meeting held, qualified opportunity and won deal are different outcomes. Track each.
5. **Make the live coach quiet enough to use.** One short utterance and one question at most. No cue is often the right cue while the buyer is explaining.
6. **Preserve clear boundaries.** One respectful clarification is available for ambiguous resistance; a firm refusal ends the pitch. No fabricated familiarity, borrowed customer results or unsupported promises.

The engine architecture is suitable for this separation:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Approved offering and ICP] --> B[Research evidence]
    B --> C[Combined research skill]
    C --> D[Brief with facts and hypotheses]
    D --> E[Human call and transcript]
    E --> F[Combined live coach]
    F --> G[Reviewed next step and CRM update]
    G --> H[Meeting held and opportunity outcomes]
    H --> A
```

The next engineering priorities are better source retrieval, semantic evidence checks, and live cue latency. The current research path uses short snippets; a better skill cannot infer information that never reaches it. Qualification now requires contact-speech grounding, but matching words do not prove the criterion's meaning. DNC is latched for the call, and review actions enforce field/kind limits. Calendar availability can still delay a turn. [Engine assessment](engine-assessment.md) distinguishes completed fixes from remaining work.
